Part 01.b / 10 — Scope Definition

What this catalog covers. And what it deliberately does not.

In scope

Sales · Revenue · GTM · Demand

  • Market, demand, value, channel, competitive, customer & PMF discovery
  • Pipeline architecture, GTM motion design, sales execution systems
  • Revenue intelligence, forecasting, deal velocity
  • Buyer intent, ICP, segmentation, whitespace & micro-segment economics
  • Pricing, packaging & offer architecture
  • Expansion & cross-sell revenue discovery
Out of scope

Handled by other ProfitMachines practices

  • Enterprise AI governance & SOC2 / ISO programs
  • Back-office, finance & HR automation
  • Post-sale support operations (except expansion revenue)
  • General cloud, infrastructure & platform engineering
  • Security operations & zero-trust architecture
Two delivery tracks run through every offering. Track A — Consulting Services: human-led diagnostics, strategy, playbooks, enablement, governance. Track B — AI-Driven Automation Engagements: agentic systems, ML models, workflow automation, telemetry pipelines. Track A is about deciding what to do. Track B is about building the machine that does it.
Part 02 / 10 — Model Deep-Dives

The eight engagement models.

Each model below is defined by five things: how it is structured, who staffs it, what it produces, who carries the risk, and when it is the right answer. Sub-packages are pre-scoped variants that reduce sales friction and time-to-kickoff.

Seven Standard Guardrails

PoV / PoP Charter

Success metric, baseline, attribution method, and gate criteria are defined and signed before kickoff — never retrofitted.

Outcome Charter

For E5 / E6: KPIs, measurement window, data access, and client preconditions are locked pre-signature. Protects both parties.

All outcome- and risk-bearing models require CRM access, clean baseline data, and a named counterpart. If unmet, KPI obligations are void.

Attribution Rules

What counts as sourced vs. influenced is agreed before measurement begins — not negotiated after the number lands.

Conversion Credit

PoV and PoP fees credit 50–100% against a subsequent larger engagement. Proof should never be paid for twice.

Exit & Off-Ramp

Every model carries a defined exit: PoV go/no-go gate, quarterly out-clause on retainers, milestone exit on transformations.

On E4, E7 and E8, all enablement content and playbooks transfer to client IP at completion. We build machines you can own.

Part 10.b / 10 — FAQ

Frequently asked.

Can we start small and expand later?

That is the designed path. Most clients enter through a Micro-Segment Kit or an E3 Proof of Value, where 50–100% of the fee credits against the next engagement. The entire model architecture is built to let risk decrease as evidence accumulates.

How is "verified" performance actually verified?

Baseline is locked before deployment. A dedicated Revenue Analyst instruments the measurement, and for E5 / E6 engagements a Performance Auditor — independent of the delivery pod — signs off the numbers before any success fee is invoiced. Attribution rules are written into the charter before kickoff.

What happens if our data or CRM is a mess?

It is more common than not. Outcome- and risk-bearing models (E5, E6, E8 accelerators) list data access and baseline quality as contractual preconditions. Where those are unmet, we either start with an E2-A Revenue Leak Diagnostic to establish the baseline, or restructure the engagement to consulting terms until instrumentation exists.

Do you replace our sales team?

No. E8 Forward Deployed operators work alongside your reps on defined motions and segments, with a hard capability-transfer clause at completion. E7 Residents are explicitly measured on how fast the client team can run the workstream without us.

We are not a SaaS company. Does this still apply?

Yes. The catalog is segmented by product-line economics — Services, Retail & Commerce, Platform — because the revenue mechanics differ structurally between selling people-hours, transactions, and usage. The discovery layers are constant; the emphasis and engagement design are not.

How fast can something start?

E1 Advisory and E8 FDA can start inside one week. E3 PoV typically kicks off within two weeks of charter signature. E4 Transformation programs take three to four weeks to mobilize properly — rushing that phase is the most common cause of adoption failure.

What is the smallest engagement you sell?

A Micro-Segment Kit — three weeks, fixed scope, fixed fee — or an E1-D War-Room Advisory of one to two weeks. Both exist to answer one question decisively.
