## 3. Consistency & Validation Rules
1. **No Filler Text:** Every section must deliver dense, actionable analytical value. If an explanation is completed in fewer words, use the remainder for detailed quantitative scenarios rather than circular prose.
2. **Mathematical Cohesion:** The baseline enterprise model ($100M ARR enterprise) established in Chapter 5 and 6 must flow directly into the TCO, NPV, IRR, and ROI calculations in Chapters 10, 11, and 12.
3. **Traceability:** Every chapter must explicitly reference the relevant ProfitMachines architectural offerings and connect operational metrics (e.g., lead response latency) to financial balance sheet outcomes (e.g., EBITDA margin expansion).
