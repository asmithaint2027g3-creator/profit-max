## 3. Quantified Revenue & Operational Benefits

### Benefit Vector A: Active Selling Time Expansion (+35% Productive Hours)
- Baseline active selling time: 32% (12.8 hrs/week).
- Automated CRM hygiene, meeting intelligence synthesis, AI prep: +5.5 hrs/week.
- Post-transformation active selling time: ~43% (17.2 hrs/week).
- Impact: 15% increase in pipeline capacity without adding headcount.

### Benefit Vector B: Win Rate Uplift (+16% Relative)
- Win rate improves from 21.5% to 25.0% through SPICED qualification rigor and MEDDPICC deal defense.
- Direct revenue contribution on $100M run-rate: +$3.2M ARR in Year 1, +$4.8M in Year 2, +$6.1M in Year 3.

### Benefit Vector C: Sales Cycle Velocity Compression (-18 Days)
- Sales cycle contracts from 94 days to 76 days (-19.1%).
- Working capital release and pipeline velocity acceleration: +$1.4M ARR equivalent annual velocity benefit.

### Benefit Vector D: NRR Expansion (+350 bps)
- Churn early-warning indicators and automated cross-sell propensity scoring increase NRR from 102% to 105.5%.
- Preserved and expanded existing customer ARR: +$1.8M in Year 1, +$3.6M in Year 2, +$5.5M in Year 3.
